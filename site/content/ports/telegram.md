# telegram

a palette for telegram desktop. the chat list and folders take the darker chrome shade, received messages the popup shade and your own a blue tint.

{{downloads}}

## install

send `{{slug}}.tdesktop-palette` to your saved messages and click it, or pick it with **settings → chat settings → chat background → choose from file**. telegram shows a preview; apply it from there.

## notes

- a palette carries no chat background, so telegram keeps your wallpaper. for a plain one, open **chat background** and pick a solid color near the theme's background.
- names in groups take the eight accents in telegram's order: red, green, yellow, blue, violet, pink, cyan and steel.
